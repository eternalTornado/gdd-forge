# Cultural Design Guidelines — East Asian Markets (China, Japan, South Korea)

## Regional Market Preferences

### East Asian Markets

#### China
**Cultural Values**:
- Collectivism and community harmony
- Respect for hierarchy and authority
- Face-saving and social status
- Long-term thinking and patience
- Family and generational connections

**Gaming Preferences**:
- Social and community-focused gameplay
- Collection and completion mechanics
- Status display and achievement systems
- Cooperative rather than competitive emphasis
- Progression systems with clear milestones

**Visual Design Preferences**:
- Rich, detailed artwork with symbolic meaning
- Gold and red color associations with prosperity
- Symmetrical and balanced compositions
- Traditional cultural motifs when appropriate
- High production value and polish

**Monetization Preferences**:
- Gacha and collection-based systems
- Social gifting and sharing features
- Status symbol purchases
- Long-term investment in character/account
- Community-based spending incentives

**Content Considerations**:
- Avoid political references or sensitive topics
- Respect for traditional values and customs
- Positive portrayal of authority figures
- Emphasis on harmony and cooperation
- Cultural festivals and seasonal events

#### Japan
**Cultural Values**:
- Attention to detail and craftsmanship
- Respect for tradition and innovation balance
- Group harmony (wa) and consensus
- Seasonal awareness and appreciation
- Aesthetic beauty and refinement

**Gaming Preferences**:
- Character-driven narratives and development
- Seasonal events and limited-time content
- Collection and customization systems
- High-quality audio and visual presentation
- Nostalgic and emotional storytelling

**Visual Design Preferences**:
- Clean, minimalist design with attention to detail
- Seasonal color palettes and themes
- Character design with emotional expressiveness
- Traditional and modern aesthetic fusion
- Subtle animation and micro-interactions

**Monetization Preferences**:
- Gacha systems with guaranteed outcomes
- Character and costume collection
- Seasonal and limited-time offers
- Premium quality content and experiences
- Respectful and non-aggressive monetization

**Content Considerations**:
- Seasonal celebrations and cultural events
- Respect for traditional values and customs
- Emotional depth in character relationships
- Attention to cultural nuances and etiquette
- Appreciation for craftsmanship and quality

#### South Korea
**Cultural Values**:
- Competitive achievement and excellence
- Social hierarchy and respect for elders
- Technology adoption and innovation
- Community and group identity
- Education and skill development

**Gaming Preferences**:
- Competitive and ranking-based systems
- Guild and clan-based social features
- Skill-based progression and mastery
- Streaming and content creation integration
- Real-time competitive gameplay

**Visual Design Preferences**:
- Modern, sleek, and technologically advanced
- Bright, vibrant color schemes
- Character designs with strong personality
- UI designs that emphasize functionality
- Integration with social media aesthetics

**Monetization Preferences**:
- Competitive advantage items (within limits)
- Social status and ranking displays
- Guild and community-based purchases
- Streaming and content creation tools
- Premium convenience features

**Content Considerations**:
- Competitive tournaments and esports integration
- Social media and streaming features
- Respect for hierarchical relationships
- Technology and innovation themes
- Community achievement recognition
