# Cultural Design Guidelines — Emerging Markets (Southeast Asia, Latin America, India)

### Emerging Markets

**Cultural Values**:
- Family and community importance
- Respect for elders and authority
- Religious and spiritual awareness
- Economic pragmatism
- Cultural diversity appreciation

**Gaming Preferences**:
- Social and family-friendly gameplay
- Mobile-first experiences
- Affordable and accessible content
- Community and guild features
- Cultural celebration integration

**Visual Design Preferences**:
- Colorful and vibrant aesthetics
- Cultural motifs and traditional elements
- Mobile-optimized interface design
- Celebration and festival themes
- Family and community imagery

**Monetization Preferences**:
- Affordable pricing tiers
- Local payment method integration
- Social and community-based monetization
- Cultural event-based offers
- Family-friendly spending options
