# Monetization Models — Platform-Specific Considerations

## Platform-Specific Considerations

### Mobile Monetization
**Primary Models**: F2P with IAP, Ad-supported
**Key Metrics**: ARPU, LTV, Retention curves
**Common Strategies**:
- Energy/lives systems
- Cosmetic purchases
- Convenience items
- Rewarded video ads
- Subscription options

**Mobile-Specific Features**:
- Small transaction amounts ($0.99-$9.99)
- Impulse purchase optimization
- Social sharing incentives
- Push notification integration
- Platform-specific payment methods

### PC Monetization
**Primary Models**: Premium, F2P, Subscription
**Key Platforms**: Steam, Epic, GOG, Direct
**Common Strategies**:
- Early access programs
- Seasonal sales participation
- Community market integration
- Mod support monetization
- Collector's editions

**PC-Specific Features**:
- Higher price tolerance
- DLC expansion expectations
- Community-driven content
- Platform achievement integration
- Regional pricing strategies

### Console Monetization
**Primary Models**: Premium + DLC, F2P (growing)
**Key Platforms**: PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo
**Common Strategies**:
- Season pass models
- Collector's editions
- Platform-exclusive content
- Subscription service inclusion
- Cross-platform progression

**Console-Specific Features**:
- Platform certification requirements
- First-party promotion opportunities
- Physical/digital split considerations
- Platform-specific payment systems
- Achievement/trophy integration
