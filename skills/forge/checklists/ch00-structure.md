# Checklist ch00-structure — Structural & Payoff Baseline (Chapters 3 & 4)
Applies to: 3_Game Overview.md, 4_Gameplay and Mechanics.md · Used by: gdd-concept-architect, gdd-mechanics-designer (self-check) and gdd-reviewer.

## Core Loop & Pillars
- [ ] ST-01 Core gameplay loop is stated as a named cycle with explicit verbs (not just described abstractly)
- [ ] ST-02 Design pillars (3-5) are each named, given one sentence, and state what they forbid
- [ ] ST-03 Every mechanic in ch4 cites which pillar it serves
- [ ] ST-04 Core Thrill Loop (session loop) and Meta Loop (progression loop) are each written as separate stated cycles, not merged
- [ ] ST-05 World rules & physics are documented in ch4 §4.3 before any level-specific content appears

## Progression & Escalation
- [ ] ST-06 Progression structure names how challenge/complexity increases over the course of play (not just "gets harder")
- [ ] ST-07 A point where a major mechanic or system is added mid-progression is identified
- [ ] ST-08 Endgame/mastery state is described with what "success" looks like at the end

## Mechanics Setup & Payoff
- [ ] ST-09 Every mechanic introduced in the Mechanics Catalogue (§4.4) is referenced again in at least one later section (economy, levels, or balance) — no mechanic is written once and abandoned
- [ ] ST-10 Tutorial/onboarding order (ch4 §4.4 or ch6 §6.3) states which mechanic is taught first and why
- [ ] ST-11 Unlockable/progression content states the gameplay purpose it serves, not only that it unlocks

## Document Completeness
- [ ] ST-12 3_Game Overview.md contains all ten sections (§3.1–§3.10) listed in its template
- [ ] ST-13 4_Gameplay and Mechanics.md contains all eleven sections (§4.1–§4.11) listed in its template, or a stated Depth-rule reason for omission
- [ ] ST-14 Every section ends where the template says it ends — no section merges two numbered headings into one

## Anti-fabrication
- [ ] ST-15 No feature, mechanic, or system claims a platform, audience, or business model not present in brief.md
- [ ] ST-16 No pillar is invented from a genre convention alone; every pillar traces to D-02, D-08 or D-43
- [ ] ST-17 Difficulty/economy claims use "(tunable)" or "(est.)" tags rather than asserting fixed numbers as fact

## Consistency rules
- [ ] ST-18 [Rule 2] Every feature in ch4 §4.11 traces to a pillar in ch3 §3.2 and appears in ch12's roadmap
- [ ] ST-19 [Rule 6] Every UNDECIDED field touched by ch3 or ch4 appears in that chapter's Open Decisions box
