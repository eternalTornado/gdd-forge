# Checklist ch05-world — World Design Continuity & Cultural Sensitivity
Applies to: 5_Story, Setting and Character.md · Used by: gdd-narrative-designer (self-check) and gdd-reviewer.
`[R]` = reviewer-only — depends on a chapter written after this one; at self-check count it as n/a (exclude from passed/total), never as a fail.

## Geography & Systems
- [ ] WO-01 Level geography referenced in §5.3 World Map & Regions is consistent with any other mention of the same region in the chapter
- [ ] WO-02 World Map & Regions table has purpose, mood, gameplay role, and connections filled for every region
- [ ] WO-03 Setting rules stated in §5.2 do not contradict World Rules & Physics defined in ch4 §4.3
- [ ] WO-04 Travel/movement between regions described is plausible given the connections stated in the World Map table
- [ ] WO-05 Environmental storytelling hooks (§5.9) are tied to specific regions or levels, not stated generically

## Cultural Sensitivity
- [ ] WO-06 Cultural & Sensitivity Notes (§5.10) name the specific D-05 region(s) they address
- [ ] WO-07 Cultural symbols, religious content, or historical references used in the setting are flagged for sensitivity review, not asserted without qualification
- [ ] WO-08 No region, faction, or culture in the setting is depicted as a stereotype without a stated design rationale
- [ ] WO-09 Cultural or historical claims used to justify setting details cite the `data/cultural/` slice you were given or brief.md

## Factions & Timeline
- [ ] WO-10 Factions & Organisations (§5.7) states each faction's relation to at least one other faction or to the protagonist
- [ ] WO-11 Backstory & Timeline (§5.4) is internally ordered (no event referenced before its stated cause)

## Anti-fabrication
- [ ] WO-12 No real-world culture, religion, or historical event is used as direct source material without being named as reference material, not fact
- [ ] WO-13 No world detail contradicts a D-33 narrative given

## Consistency rules
- [ ] WO-14 [Rule 1] World/region terms match the Glossary in ch3 §3.10
- [ ] WO-15 [Rule 6] Any UNDECIDED field touched (e.g. D-38 localisation targets affecting region names) is listed in the Open Decisions box
