  - §5.1 Platform & Device Floor — `D-04` platform list, minimum device/browser target from `D-37` (or `UNDECIDED` verbatim).

If `D-11 = UNDECIDED`, engine-specific rows read "engine-dependent," identical to the full profile's Depth rule. If `D-42` lists existing tooling, integrate it rather than proposing a parallel new tool.
  - §5.8 Technical Risks — engineering-specific risks (not business/schedule risk — that belongs to `4_Business and LiveOps.md` §4.7), each phrased so `4_Business and LiveOps.md`'s risk register could lift it directly.
  - Open Decisions — one bullet per `UNDECIDED` brief field and GAP. `D-11 = UNDECIDED` and `D-47 = UNDECIDED` always appear here per the Depth rule.
