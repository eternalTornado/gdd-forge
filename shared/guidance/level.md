# Guidance — level systems

Level or world structure, pacing, difficulty curve, procedural rules.

- D-23 decides the structure: linear levels, hub, open world, procedural, level select, endless. Never switch it because another structure "flows better".
- Every level or zone introduces or tests a mechanic that exists in a mechanics doc, by name. A level that exists only "for pacing" has no reason to exist.
- Introduce one new mechanic at a time, in the order the mechanics docs make learnable.
- Pacing is a curve: describe tension rising and easing across the level list, not a difficulty number per row.
- Keep one Content table as the single list of levels and their order; target duration scales from D-09 session length and is marked (est.).
- Layout notes show gates, branches and the critical path, not room-by-room art.
- Procedural rules are constraints a generator follows ("corridors never exceed 3 branches"), not intent ("varied and interesting"). Keep every generator tunable in one table.
- Asset needs per level are categories ("desert ruins kit, 2 new enemy silhouettes"), not final asset names.
