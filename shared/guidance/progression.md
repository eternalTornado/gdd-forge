# Guidance — progression systems

Unlocks, levels, power or skill growth, meta loop.

- Size progression to D-09: a Micro-scope game gets one progression track, not a 60-hour unlock tree.
- Say what grows: player skill, character power, or content access. Mixing all three without saying which drives retention makes balance unclear.
- Every unlock needs a trigger the player can see coming ("reach level 5", "clear world 2"), stated as a rule.
- Describe curves in words ("early levels ramp gently, a spike at each world boss") and name the tunables that drive each curve; numbers live in Tunables, marked (tunable).
- Retention hooks must come from systems the game has; never invent a daily-login or battle pass the economy and D-30 do not support.
- If progression can be bought, check D-30 and D-31 for every purchasable step; "no pay-to-win" forbids selling power, not just labelling it.
- Player-Observable Behaviors should cover the first unlock, a mid-game unlock, and what happens at the cap.
