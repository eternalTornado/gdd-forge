# Review checklist — concept.md + decisions.md

Judgement items only; `gdd_check.py` already checks headings, budgets, tables and references.

1. Every pillar's Forbids rules out something real, and no Forbids blocks a must-have (D-43).
2. The Core Loop is a verb cycle the player performs, consistent with the genre (D-03) and scope (D-09).
3. Every system in § Systems serves a pillar, and the core-tier systems are enough to run the core loop and no more.
4. Nothing in the concept contradicts a `user` decision, and every choice the concept relies on is a row in `decisions.md`.
5. Proposals in `decisions.md` are plausible for the pitch; none quietly overrides something the pitch implied.
6. Out of Scope covers D-44 and the things a reader would otherwise expect from the genre.
7. Glossary terms are the ones systems will reuse; no near-duplicates.
8. Proportion: the concept is sized to the scope; a hyper-casual concept reads in two minutes.
